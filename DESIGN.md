# Visual Identity & UI Direction: Kural Sevi (குரல் செவி)
**Voice-First Livelihood Intelligence Platform for PM-AJAY**  
*Department of Social Justice and Empowerment (MoSJE), Government of India*

---

## 1. Brand Essence & Name Rationale

### The Name
- **Kural (குரல்)**: Classical Tamil for *"Voice"*, invoking the universal egalitarian ethics of the *Tirukkural* (*குறள்*).
- **Sevi (செவி)**: Classical Tamil for *"Ear"* or *"The one who listens with attentiveness"*.
- **Combined Meaning**: *"The Voice That Listens"* — reflecting a compassionate, bilingual AI interface that meets rural and informal Scheduled Caste (SC) workers through speech, removing literacy and smartphone barriers.

### Minimalist & Impressive Tone
- **Dignified Public Infrastructure**: Carries the institutional trust and authority of national governance (Digital India / PM-AJAY) without the visual clutter, heavy bureaucratic blocks, or loud garish gradients of default government portals.
- **Extreme Color Discipline**: Adopts a strict **3-color minimalist palette**:
  1. **Primary**: **Ashok Chakra Blue** (`#0B3064`) — Authoritative, calm, high-contrast digital governance anchor.
  2. **Secondary**: **Saffron** (`#E05A1B`) — Warm, energizing, surgical accent for priority notices, header micro-strip, and attention states.
  3. **Tertiary**: **India Green** (`#0A783C`) — Exclusively reserved for confirmed, verified, and sanctioned states.
- **Calm Off-White Canvas**: Slate-50 (`#F8FAFC`) background with pure white surfaces, preventing eye fatigue across long administrative shifts.

## 3. Double-Channel Accessibility (Red-Green Colorblindness Defense)

To ensure fail-safe operation for officers with deuteranopia or protanopia:
1. **Never Color Alone**: Every badge, status indicator, and card pairs color with a distinct **Lucide icon** AND a **text label**.
2. **Confidence Scored Without Confusion**:
   - **High Match (90%+)**: Ashok Chakra Blue (`#0B3064` on `#EAF1FB`) + dot + *"High Confidence"*
   - **Medium Match**: Neutral Slate (`#475569` on `#F1F5F9`) + dot + *"Medium Confidence"*
   - **Review Required**: Saffron (`#C24810` on `#FFF4ED`) + dot + *"Review Required"*
   - **Green is preserved exclusively** for official legal sanction approval.

## 4. Typography System

- **Latin & Numerals**: **DM Sans** (Google Fonts) with `font-feature-settings: 'tnum'` for financial, operational, and case metrics.
- **Indic Script Stack**: **Noto Sans Tamil**, **Noto Sans Telugu**, and **Noto Sans Devanagari** matching baseline and weight across multilingual dossiers.
- **Monospace Identifiers**: System Monospace (`font-mono`) for case docket IDs (`KS-2026-00142`) and qualification codes (`APP/Q0301`).

---

## 5. Logo & Wordmark Direction (Flag Code of India Compliant)

- **The Mark**: An abstract soundwave circle in deep Ashok Chakra Blue (`#0B3064`) with ripples radiating **inward** toward an attentive saffron listening core (`#E05A1B`).
- **Legal Safeguard**: Strictly non-infringing under the *Flag Code of India, 2002* and the *State Emblem of India Act, 2005* (no tricolor bands, no 24-spoke wheel reproduction, no Lion Capital).
